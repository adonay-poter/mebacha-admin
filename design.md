## Overview

Operations & merchant dispatch dashboard — industrial precision with cold steel slate framing, high-density telemetry, tracking numbers in IBM Plex Mono, and Mebacha's signature Ethiopian emerald green (`#006838`) as the primary action driver.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#14181D`):** Near-black text, telemetry headers, and high-contrast titles.
- **Secondary (`#5A6470`):** Borders, captions, and operational metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#006838` - Mebacha Green):** The primary driver for dispatch, saves, plan upgrades, and approvals. Deep version `#004D2A`, soft container `#E6F4EC`.
- **Accent Gold (`#866128`):** Secondary highlight for pilots, warnings, and touchpoint milestones.
- **Neutral (`#F4F6F8`):** Freight neutral canvas.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High clarity card foundations.
